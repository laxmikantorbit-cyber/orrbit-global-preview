Privacy information

This explains the data flow on our enquiry-only custom software website. Last updated: 10 October 2026.

Who operates this website?

oRRbit™ is a software brand owned and operated by oRRbit Slickteq Softech Pvt. Ltd., conducting business under the trade name ORBIT GROUP.

What happens when you contact us?

Enquiry form entries are composed in your browser and are not submitted to an oRRbit server by this website. Only when you confirm the consent checkbox and continue does the site open a WhatsApp message containing the information you entered. You decide whether to send it. The business may receive and retain messages you choose to send in WhatsApp for enquiry response, follow-up and business records.

Hosting and third parties

This page is hosted on Render, which may process normal connection information such as IP addresses and technical access logs to serve and secure the site. WhatsApp is provided by Meta; its services are subject to their own privacy policies and terms. We do not intentionally embed analytics trackers, advertising pixels or account login on this site.

What should you avoid sending?

Please do not send passwords, customer databases, government IDs, card data, medical records or other confidential content in an initial enquiry. Separate confidentiality and data-protection arrangements may be needed for an actual software project.

Questions and requests

For questions about an enquiry message, or to ask about correction or deletion subject to applicable legal obligations, contact our business via WhatsApp sales. Deletion from independent third-party systems is subject to those providers' controls.
