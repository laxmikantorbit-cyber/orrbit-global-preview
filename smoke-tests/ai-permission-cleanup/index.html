Low-cost tools show what exists. Enterprise platforms can remediate, but are expensive and heavy. This concept targets the middle: local, explainable cleanup planning for SMB IT teams and MSPs.

Safety model

V1 is read-only by default. Deterministic Windows permission logic calculates access. AI explains, prioritizes and drafts a cleanup plan; it does not silently change ACLs.

Who it is for

Windows sysadmins, MSP technicians, IT consultants and SMB security teams inheriting years of mixed direct permissions, nested groups and undocumented access.
